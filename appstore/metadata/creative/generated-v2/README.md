# Generated Pluck marketing concepts

Created October 16, 2026 with the built-in image-generation tool. These replace the design direction of the earlier window-focused artwork: photographed source material, screen-region selection, then usable text. They are generated promotional illustrations, not recorded application screenshots or measured OCR outputs.

- `01-photo.png`: a photographed flower-market sign becomes editable text.
- `02-video.png`: words on a chalkboard in a paused video become editable text.
- `03-scan.png`: a recipe on a photographed page becomes editable text.

Each image was visually inspected: source material is visibly physical, the copied words match the selected phrase, the result is a digital text surface, and the headline and example are legible. No Pluck window or settings panel dominates the composition. Generated cursor/selection artwork is illustrative, not a pixel-exact capture of the app's overlay.

The final images remain at the image tool's native 1586 × 992 dimensions. They are review concepts, not App Store upload-ready exports. They have **not** replaced the screenshots in Apple's submitted version 1.0 (6), and the submission has not been withdrawn. Before a future Store replacement, approve the visual direction, prepare final compliant screenshot assets, and verify actual app captures and OCR results.

`prompts.json` preserves the three generation prompts; `refinement-prompt.txt` records the targeted edit that made the results visibly digital and straightened the selection regions. `manifest.json` records dimensions, bytes, and SHA-256 hashes. Initial iterations are preserved locally in `work/generated-v2-iterations` and are not committed.
